# SAP-S-4HANA-Business-Process-of-Material-Management-MM-

## Overview
This project demonstrates the **Material Management (MM)** module in SAP S/4HANA, focusing on procurement and inventory management processes. It covers the end-to-end purchasing cycle, from material creation and purchase requisition to vendor selection, purchase order creation, goods receipt, invoice verification and vendor payment.

The project explains how SAP S/4HANA integrates material management with other business functions to improve operational efficiency, data accuracy and financial transparency.

## Objectives

- Understand ERP systems and the role of SAP S/4HANA in business operations.
- Learn the core functions of the Material Management (MM) module.
- Perform procurement and inventory management transactions.
- Understand vendor quotation comparison and purchasing decisions.
- Explore integration between MM, Financial Accounting (FI), Controlling (CO) and Production Planning (PP).

## Key Processes Covered

- Creation of Material Master for trading goods.
- Stock and requirements list display.
- Purchase requisition creation.
- Request for Quotation (RFQ) creation and vendor quotation maintenance.
- Vendor price comparison and quotation selection.
- Purchase order creation and display.
- Goods receipt and stock verification.
- Invoice receipt and verification.
- Creation of purchasing information records.
- Vendor payment processing and financial document review.

## Tools and Technologies

- SAP S/4HANA ERP
- Material Management (MM)
- SAP Fiori
- SAP transaction codes, including `ME51N`, `ME41`, `ME47`, `ME49`, `ME21N`, `MIGO_GR`, `MIRO` and `ME11`

## Key Learnings

- Understanding end-to-end procurement and inventory management.
- Managing material master data and stock requirements.
- Comparing vendor quotations and selecting cost-effective suppliers.
- Processing purchase orders, goods receipts and vendor invoices.
- Understanding the integration of purchasing activities with financial accounting.
- Improving business process efficiency through ERP systems.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how SAP S/4HANA Material Management streamlines procurement and inventory operations through an integrated ERP environment. By completing the purchasing cycle, including vendor selection, goods receipt, invoice verification and payment processing, the project highlights the importance of ERP systems in maintaining accurate inventory records and supporting efficient business operations.
